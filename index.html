Method & limitations

Scores are regression-adjusted; Max PF is a relative roster-depth signal; weekly SD is measured where possible. A coverage-gated current-squad overlay uses the best legal lineup from up to three upcoming Sleeper projection weeks and is deliberately capped so it cannot erase games already lost. At least 70% of each active roster must map; any remaining lineup gap uses a visibly counted low-end positional estimate rather than zero. Provider projections can change and this overlay is not part of the historical Accuracy Lab comparison. Bye-week replacement scoring and custom brackets remain approximate; live weeks stay provisional.
