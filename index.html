🔎 Model notes & data quality

No Sleeper projections · walk-forward accuracy testing

v7 forecast: Historical points are shrunk toward the league mean until there is sufficient evidence. Max PF adds a relative roster-depth signal (not an unearned uplift to every team). Team-specific weekly score volatility uses observed results, regularised toward league variance. Completed fixtures come only from Sleeper's official standings. The original v6 method remains accessible as a comparison.

Data limitations: No live injury, betting, trade valuation or player-specific projection inputs. Bye replacements use approximate position-based effects. Bracket probabilities assume the saved Sunday Funday house rules until verified. The player directory may be cached locally for up to 24 hours. Sleeper is read-only; no trades or waivers can be made from this dashboard.
